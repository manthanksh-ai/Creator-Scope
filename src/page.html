This is a database for Indian creator campaigns built from supplied rosters and public sources. It does not represent every creator in India or prove an agency's current representation. Metrics and rates are reported snapshots; their measurement periods and validity may be unknown. Conflicting values are preserved in profile details.

Family content and having a child require public creator evidence. Audience splits stay blank until entered from Instagram Insights. No demographic estimates are generated.
